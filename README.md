# CodeAlpha_PhishingAwarenessTraining

Phishing Awareness Training for the **CodeAlpha Cybersecurity Internship, Task 2**. It teaches non-technical people how to recognize phishing, understand social engineering, and respond safely.

## Topics covered

1. Why phishing matters
2. The attack lifecycle
3. Types: email, spear phishing, whaling, smishing, vishing, quishing
4. Anatomy of a phishing email
5. Spotting fake websites
6. Smishing and vishing examples
7. Social engineering tactics
8. Real-world cases: Google/Facebook, Twitter 2020, Target 2013, BEC
9. The S.T.O.P. check (Sender, Tone, Ask, Path)
10. Best practices for individuals and organizations
11. What to do if you clicked
12. Spot-the-phish exercise and 8-question quiz
